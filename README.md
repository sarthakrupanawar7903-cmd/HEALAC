# HEALAC 🩺
### Your health, illuminated.

**HEALAC is an AI-powered personal health copilot that turns confusing medical documents into clear, understandable health insights.**

Upload a prescription, lab report, or discharge summary. HEALAC extracts the important information, explains medical terms in simple language, helps you understand your medicines, and highlights abnormal test results — all in one place.

## 💡 The Problem
Medical records are often difficult to understand and scattered across hospitals, labs, and doctors. Patients may struggle to interpret test results, understand prescriptions, or keep track of their health history.

## ✨ What HEALAC Does
- 📄 **Smart Document Analysis** — Extracts information from prescriptions, lab reports, and discharge summaries using AI.
- 🧠 **Simple Health Explanations** — Understand medical terms, test results, and what your medicines are prescribed for.
- 🚨 **Abnormal Value Alerts** — Highlights unusual lab results and explains their potential significance.
- 🗓️ **Unified Health Timeline** — Organizes medical records chronologically to make health history easier to follow.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support** — Makes health information more accessible in regional languages.
- 🩺 **Doctor-Ready Summaries** — Helps users share organized medical histories with healthcare professionals.

## ⚙️ Tech Stack
- **AI:** Google Gemini,Claude,Deepseek,OpenAI (multimodal document analysis)
- **Frontend:** React / Next.js
- **Backend:** Node.js / FastAPI
- **Database:** PostgreSQL / Firebase
- **Deployment:** Vercel 

## 🎯 Our Vision
Make healthcare information easier to understand, easier to access, and easier to manage — so people can have more informed conversations with their doctors.

> **HEALAC doesn't replace your doctor. It helps you understand your health better.**
